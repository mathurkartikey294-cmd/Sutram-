# Validated Forensic Benchmark Datasets and Models (DATASETS_AND_MODELS.md)

This document catalogs real, validated public datasets and pretrained model weights suitable for training, evaluating, and running SUTRAM's forensic detection engines.

---

## 1. Candidate Benchmark Datasets

### 1.1 Video & Facial Deepfake Datasets
1. **FaceForensics++ (FF++)**:
   - *Description*: Standard benchmark containing 1,000 original video sequences manipulated with four automated face-swap methods (Deepfakes, Face2Face, FaceSwap, NeuralTextures) at three compression qualities (raw, c23/light, c40/heavy).
   - *Use Case*: Video frame classifier benchmark, compression resilience testing.
   - *License*: Non-commercial academic research use upon request.
2. **Celeb-DF (v2)**:
   - *Description*: 5,639 high-quality deepfake video clips generated from YouTube interview videos of celebrities, designed to eliminate standard visual artifacts (color mismatches, edge flickering).
   - *Use Case*: Advanced facial boundary blending and rPPG pulse evaluation.
   - *License*: Academic research license upon application.
3. **Deepfake Detection Challenge (DFDC)**:
   - *Description*: Over 100,000 video clips created by Meta/AWS with diverse actors, lighting, and synthetic modifications.
   - *Use Case*: Out-of-distribution robustness evaluation.
   - *License*: DFDC Dataset Agreement.

### 1.2 Audio & Voice Cloning Datasets
1. **ASVspoof 2019 / 2021 (Automatic Speaker Verification and Spoofing)**:
   - *Description*: Gold-standard benchmark for speech synthesis (TTS) and voice conversion (VC) detection across logical access (LA) and physical access (PA) scenarios.
   - *Use Case*: Benchmarking `wav2vec_classifier.py` and prosodic feature extraction.
   - *License*: Open for academic research (Interspeech consortium).
2. **WaveFake Dataset**:
   - *Description*: Multi-architecture synthetic audio dataset containing recordings generated by MelGAN, Parallel WaveGAN, HiFi-GAN, WaveGlow, and Multi-band MelGAN across English and Japanese.
   - *Use Case*: Spectral high-frequency rolloff and vocoder artifact evaluation.
   - *License*: Open-access (Creative Commons).

### 1.3 Image Tampering & Splicing Datasets
1. **CASIA Image Tampering Dataset (v1 and v2)**:
   - *Description*: Spliced and copy-move manipulated images with corresponding ground-truth binary tampering masks.
   - *Use Case*: Evaluating `copy_move.py`, `ela.py`, and `splicing.py`.
   - *License*: Academic use.
2. **Columbia Image Splicing Detection Dataset**:
   - *Description*: Uncompressed authentic and spliced image pairs focusing on sharp boundary discontinuities.
   - *Use Case*: Evaluating edge transition and noise variance consistency.

## 3. Storage and Weight Management Policy

- **No Model Weights in Git**: Binary model weight files (`.pt`, `.onnx`, `.safetensors`, `.bin`) are explicitly excluded via `.gitignore`.
- **Download Automation**: All model downloads must be handled via `scripts/download_models.py`, which validates SHA-256 checksums before placing files into `models_cache/`.
- **Graceful Fallbacks**: If model weights are missing, the system defaults to deterministic benchmark fixtures (when `MOCK_MODE=true`) or returns `status="not_implemented"`. Under no circumstances will the platform crash or return ungrounded random numbers.
